Methodology & Interpretation

Read the analysis responsibly

Meaningful comparisons depend on consistent units, time periods, and definitions. Keep these checks in mind when interpreting the charts.

Check the measure

Read chart titles, axes, legends, and tooltips. Confirm fuel type, currency, unit, and whether a value represents price, tax, or percentage.

Compare like with like

Check countries and dates are comparable. Missing values, different tax structures, and reporting changes may affect interpretation.

Separate pattern from cause

A chart may show association, but it does not alone prove that taxation caused a price change. Causal claims need additional evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guide

What is the objective of this project?

To explore global fuel-price and taxation trends from 2020 to 2026 through interactive Tableau views and country comparisons.

Why can fuel prices differ between countries?

Differences may reflect taxes, subsidies, exchange rates, transport and distribution costs, local policy, and international supply conditions.

Why is the Tableau visualization taking time to load?

The views are hosted by Tableau Public and require an internet connection. Refresh the page or use the direct Tableau link if the embed remains blank.

Can I interact with the charts?

Yes, where supported by the published view. Try selecting marks, hovering for tooltips, or using available filters and story navigation.

Does this project prove that taxes cause higher fuel prices?

No. The visualizations support comparisons and pattern-finding, but causal conclusions require additional evidence and context.
